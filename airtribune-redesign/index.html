CONCEPT

Airtribune
redesign

An unofficial design concept for airtribune.com, the live-tracking and competition platform for paragliding. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Airtribune. Event names and dates come from the public site; tasks, tracks and races are simulated.

01 · EVENTS

Season calendar

Race-series world calendar: photo hero, live countdown, SRS spotlight, calendar strip, world tour map, timeline and a race replay with podium for every round.

OPEN → 02 · LIVE

Live tracker

SailGP-style race viewer for an SRS task: nation-coloured pilots, re-sorting leaderboard, telemetry, follow-cam, 3D tilt and race feed.

OPEN → 03 · SRS

SRS event pages

One page per Sports-Class Racing Series 2027 round, built from the official event details, with photography from SRS Tapalpa 2026.

OPEN → 04 · FEED

Live feed

The activity feed rebuilt: live flight card, animated tracks on topographic maps, altitude profiles, filters and search, light and dark themes.

OPEN → 06 · LOGO

Logo study

Final: Heritage. One shape from today's grid, red and blue kept, and a lowercase wordmark with triangle dots on the i's.

OPEN → 05 · STUDY

Thumbnail options

Four paragliding-accurate thumbnail styles compared: task map, GPS tracklog, barogram and live race.
