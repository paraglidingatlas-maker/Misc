# Voice-over studio: research notes (27 Nov 2026)

These notes come from a web research pass, summarised here. Sources are listed with each point. Use them when refining the concept.

## What broadcasters show
- **SailGP LiveLineFX:** information comes in layers. Newcomers get the leader, speed and catch-up time; deeper data is for core fans. The course boundary and marks are always drawn, and "ladder lines" show progress. https://www.tvbeurope.com/live-production/sailgps-livelinefx-charts-a-new-course-in-on-screen-graphics · https://www.svgeurope.org/blog/headlines/ai-data-augmented-graphics-and-more-sailgp-embraces-complexity-as-it-prepares-for-portsmouth-debut/
- **F1:** the timing tower shows the interval to the car ahead. Ghost cars put two traces on top of each other to show where time was won. https://fansided.com/2022/07/27/f1-intervals-explained-interval-mean-formula-1 · https://www.autosport.com/f1/news/how-the-incredibly-tight-f1-2025-is-told-through-ghost-cars/10759038/
- **Formula E:** an "as it stands" top 3. Showing *what* without explaining *why* confuses viewers. https://motorsportmagazine.com/articles/single-seaters/formula-e/how-formula-es-lack-of-identity-continues-to-hurt-its-racing
- **Tour de France (NBC):** fixed screen zones. Groups are named (breakaway, peloton) with a time gap, and a rider card appears only when that rider is on screen. https://www.sportsvideo.org/2025/07/23/how-nbc-sports-tells-the-story-of-logistically-complex-tour-de-france/
- **Ocean Race:** rank by distance to finish, with the gap to the leader. https://www.yacht.de/en/regatta/the-ocean-race/the-ocean-race-atlantic-a-quick-check-using-the-live-tracker/
- **Red Bull X-Alps tracker:** the top 5 by default; each card shows distance to goal, next turnpoint and altitude. https://www.redbullxalps.com/int-en/3d-live-tracking-features
- **Para Replay:** a drop line plus a ground shadow makes height readable on a map. https://para-replay.pages.dev/en
- **Sailplane Grand Prix:** the closest analogue to this project (3D tracking with commentary). https://sgp.aero/news/sgp-germany-surpasses-1-million-views-at-farrenberg

## Voice-over and teleprompter
- **Pace:** 125–150 words per minute, so 3 minutes is about 400–450 words. TV news uses about 3 words per second. Don't describe what the picture already shows. https://larryjordan.com/articles/counting-words-for-voice-over-timing/ · https://us.humankinetics.com/blogs/excerpt/tips-for-writing-for-broadcast
- **Prompter layout:** sans-serif, sentence case, 6–8 words per line, generous line spacing. Cues go in caps in brackets, e.g. (PAUSE), and names get phonetic spellings with the stressed syllable in caps. https://www.teleprompter.com/blog/how-to-write-a-script-for-a-teleprompter
- **Open on the decisive moment.** Retention is judged at 30 s. https://vidiq.com/blog/post/increase-audience-retention-youtube/
- **OBS:** record the mic on its own track so the voice-over can be redone. https://obsproject.com/kb/multiple-audio-track-recording-guide

## Not found
- No standard length for a daily highlights video; 2–5 minutes is an assumption.
- No PWC graphics spec, and no "auto race report" tools.
