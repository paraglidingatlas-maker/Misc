# The host's narration voice (for writing voice-over scripts)

Taken from the transcripts of his own solo narration in the PWC Superfinal 2026 Task 1 highlights and the SRS Tapalpa Task 1 and Task 3 highlights (Paragliding Atlas channel).

- **Opening:** "Hello and welcome to task X of …, right here in <place>. I'm your host Aninder Singh …" He offers "deferred live but exclusive racing action", then "So without further ado, let's dive right in (and soak in all the action)."
- **Sentences:** long and flowing, joined with "as", "while", "although" and "and". They are not clipped.
- **Pilots:** named with their nationality or role ("fellow Frenchman Baptiste", "the world number one", "the local Spanish pilots").
- **Numbers:** rounded and spoken ("with just over 30 km to go", "closing in at the 40 km mark"). No exact clock times or metres.
- **Race words:** "keeping things interesting at the front", "attack mode", "crack a bit", "find his rhythm", "split into two distinct gaggles", "saving a bit of drama for the last", "at this juncture", "needless to say", "tricky is just a word in a dictionary".
- **Wrap-up:** "To sum it up …"; "the heartbreak of landing between ESS and goal"; "What a day, what a day of racing, ladies and gentlemen"; "don't forget to hit like and subscribe, and I'll see you tomorrow".
- **Asides:** he talks to the audience ("guys", "ladies and gentlemen").
