SRS St. André les Alpes 2027St André les Alpes, France

St André offers big Alpine air in a classic venue. A mix of flatland options to the west, ridges to the north and alpine adventures to the south.

Big alpine air

The last round of the 2027 series, at one of Europe's classic race venues.

Routes in every direction from Le Chalvet: north to Briançon and Allos, the 115 km out-and-return to Fort Dormillouse, south to Col de Bleyne.
